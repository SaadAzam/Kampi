How to play Pick left or right before the timer ends. As kicker, beat the keeper’s guess to score. As keeper, match the shot to save. Choices stay hidden until the reveal. After three kicks each, a tie goes to paired sudden-death kicks. Missing your kick counts as a miss; a keeper timeout lets a valid shot score. Leaving an active match forfeits it. Chips are virtual.
